COMPUTATIONAL MODELS / HUMAN PERCEPTION

RESEARCH QUESTION

Can a machine
see like a human?

I investigate whether statistical descriptions of visual textures can predict how people perceive differences between visualizations.

The broader goal is to understand where ideal computational observers diverge from human observers — and how perceptual noise can be modeled rather than treated as unexplained error.
